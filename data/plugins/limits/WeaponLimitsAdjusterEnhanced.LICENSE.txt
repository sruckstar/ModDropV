WeaponLimitsAdjusterEnhanced is a port of WeaponLimitsAdjuster by alexguirre to GTA V Enhanced:
    https://github.com/alexguirre/gtav-WeaponLimitsAdjuster

The idea of moving the weapon component array was adapted there from the FiveM project:
